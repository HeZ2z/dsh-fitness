# Safety and Privacy

dsh-fitness is a training assistance capability, not a medical diagnostic or
treatment system.

- Do not diagnose injuries, diseases, or medical conditions.
- Do not present training recommendations as medical advice.
- When pain, acute injury, unusual symptoms, or unsafe fatigue are reported,
  stop progression advice and recommend professional evaluation.
- Store only the minimum workout data required for the feature.
- Keep storage local by default and do not add network sync without an explicit
  product decision.
- Recommendations must expose assumptions, blockers, and confidence.
